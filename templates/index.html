What is an exoplanet?

An exoplanet is a planet outside our Solar System. These distant worlds help astronomers understand how planetary systems form and evolve.

How do we find exoplanets?

Transit
A planet passing in front of its star causes a small dip in the light we observe.
Radial velocity
A planet's gravity makes its star wobble. Astronomers detect this motion through shifts in the star's spectrum.
